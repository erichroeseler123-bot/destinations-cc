# Alaska Image Assets & Provenance Catalog (`public/images/alaska/*`)

This directory contains 28 curated, rights-verified photographs of Southeast Alaska ports, harbors, glaciers, wildlife, and shore excursion environments, plus 3 portfolio WebP assets in `../portfolio/` (see `../SOURCES.md` for the combined 31-asset master index).

All imagery adheres strictly to U.S. Federal Public Domain declarations, CC0 dedications, or documented Creative Commons attribution licenses verified in `docs/portfolio-image-manifest.json`. Zero images have been scraped from Online Travel Agencies (OTAs), cruise lines, or unauthorized commercial databases, and zero images claim or depict fake operator logos.
