## 1. Operating Model & Market Structure
There are only three commercial helicopter flight companies operating guided glacier landings in Juneau:
1. **TEMSCO Helicopters** (Operating from Juneau Heliport, Egan Dr / Juneau Airport)
2. **Coastal Helicopters** (Operating from Juneau International Airport Heliport)
3. **NorthStar Trekking** (Operating from Juneau Heliport / Douglas Base)

Major cruise lines calling in Juneau (Princess Cruises, Holland America Line, Royal Caribbean, Celebrity Cruises, Norwegian Cruise Line, Disney, Carnival) do not own, maintain, or pilot aircraft in Alaska. They charter contract allocations from these three local companies and resell those seats through onboard excursion desks and pre-cruise booking engines.

## 3. Dynamic Yield & Pricing Rules

### Why Prices Are Not Static
Cruise line shore excursion desks utilize closed, dynamic yield-management systems accessible only behind passenger reservation numbers (e.g. Princess Cruise Personalizer, HAL Navigator app, Royal Caribbean Cruise Planner). Prices fluctuate based on:
1. **Ship Class & Sailing Week:** Peak July sailings frequently command higher excursion surcharges than May or September shoulder voyages.
2. **Onboard Markup Multipliers:** Cruise lines typically apply a 20% to 35% margin over their wholesale operator contracts to cover onboard excursion desk staffing, port landing fees, and shipboard credits.
3. **Operator Tariffs:** Direct operator prices reflect standard FAA Part 135 retail tariff rates filed for the operating season.
