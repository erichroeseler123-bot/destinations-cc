# Editorial voice and authorship

## Welcome to New Orleans Tours

Helpful tour concierge: compare experiences by group fit, time, transportation and weather exposure. Identify the operator and distinguish recommendations from confirmed operator facts.

## Shared writing standard

- Lead with the visitor’s decision. Use concrete details and ordinary language.
- Explain who an option suits, who it may not suit, and why.
- Remove filler such as “unforgettable adventure,” “hidden gems,” “ultimate guide,” “local intel,” “premier” and “seamless” unless there is a precise reason to use it. Do not substitute a different stock phrase.
- Never invent firsthand experience, quotes, credentials, customer stories or guarantees.
- Distinguish sourced facts, operator rules, estimates and editorial opinions. Retain relevant source links.
- Preserve route identifiers, booking links, canonical URLs, prices, policies and existing H1s during prose edits unless a separately authorized correction requires changing them.
- Match photos to the actual experience and make alt text describe the visible photo.
