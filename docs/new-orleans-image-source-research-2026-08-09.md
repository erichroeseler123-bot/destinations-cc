## Permission gate

Before any image from an operator site is copied into `public/` or used in production, record one of the following:

1. explicit affiliate/media terms allowing participating affiliates to reuse product photography;
2. written operator permission;
3. written FareHarbor/partner-program permission that clearly covers the asset and intended use; or
4. a separate license that permits the use.

A public webpage, image URL, booking relationship, or FareHarbor affiliate relationship alone should not be treated as sufficient evidence of copyright permission.
